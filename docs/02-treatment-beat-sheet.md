# HireHouse — Launch Film · Treatment & Beat Sheet

60 fps · 120 BPM · 1 beat = 30 f · 1 bar = 120 f · 30.0 s = 1800 f = 15 bars.

Ground rotation (never repeat adjacent, blue once): **black → black(3D) → white → lime → black → BLUE → white**

---

## ACT I — THE NOISE (3D)

### S1 · The Pile — 0:00.0–0:04.0 · f 0–240 · bars 1–2 · black · 3D
Black. A mono UI counter ticks in the lower left: `QA ENGINEER · APPLICANTS 0000 → 0312`.
White CV cards begin to fall through frame in 3D, first a few, then a blizzard, stacking into a
mountain. The camera drifts down and pushes in. Flat, unlit paper: white cards, grey hairline "text".
- Supers: **"One role."** (beat 2) → **"312 applications."** (bar 2)
- SFX: paper flutter building to a torrent, low sub drone; tick on counter.
- Music: intro — pulse, sparse, tension.

### S2 · The Filter — 0:04.0–0:08.0 · f 240–480 · bars 3–4 · black · 3D
A thin white scan line labelled `[ KEYWORD FILTER ]` sweeps down through the pile. Every card it
touches turns **grey-block** (the incumbent colour) and drops out of the light into black. Nine in
ten are gone. The survivors hang in the dark.
- Super: **"Most are never read."**
- SFX: electrical scan sweep; cards tumbling; a hollow thud.
- Music: filter-down / muffled, the floor drops out.

## ACT II — THE SYSTEM (3D → 2D)

### S3 · The Read — 0:08.0–0:12.0 · f 480–720 · bars 5–6 · black → white · 3D → 2D
Silence for one beat. The **blue tie** drops into frame from above, swings once like a plumb line
and settles. Where it points, the grey cards lift back up and turn white: every one of them is read.
Then the hero move: the pile **reorganises into a single ranked column**, the camera rotates to dead
perpendicular, depth flattens, and a hard bar wipe turns the ground white. The cards are now rows.
- Super: **"Every application, read."**
- SFX: fabric swish on the tie, one deep "tock" as it settles, a rising shimmer as cards lift,
  a stacked-card snap cascade as they sort, whoosh into the wipe.
- Music: the beat drops in on bar 6.

### S4 · Ranked on merit — 0:12.0–0:16.0 · f 720–960 · bars 7–8 · white · 2D UI
Product mock (black UI frame, the kit's Table). `Applicants · QA Engineer`: Candidate / Stage / Fit /
Score. Scores count up in JetBrains Mono, rows **re-sort by score** (FLIP), rank 1 lifts into a
blue `Invited` badge. Left column: chips `[How it works][Ranked]`, headline.
- Super: **"Ranked on merit, not keywords."**
- SFX: soft digital ticks on count-ups, card-slide on re-sort, click on badge.

### S5 · Interviewed for real — 0:16.0–0:20.0 · f 960–1200 · bars 9–10 · lime · 2D UI
Lime ground, black interview frame: window chrome, `CANDIDATE` eyebrow, question prompt,
REC dot + mono timer, `Anti-cheat monitoring` switch flips on (blue), per-axis meters fill in lime
on a true 0–100 scale, dark alert: `✦ Session recorded per question and checked for integrity.`
- Super: **"Interviewed for real."**
- SFX: record beep, switch click, meter fills.

## ACT III — THE DECISION

### S6 · The Shortlist — 0:20.0–0:24.0 · f 1200–1440 · bars 11–12 · black · 2D bleed
The kit's hero layout: four staggered cards bleeding off the bottom — **lime, lilac, yellow, blue**
— each a shortlisted candidate (monogram avatar, name, role, score). The black toast slides in:
`Interview invite sent ✓` (lime check).
- Super: **"A shortlist, not a spreadsheet."** then **"You make the call."**
- SFX: four rising card hits on the beat, toast pop.

### S7 · Climax — 0:24.0–0:27.0 · f 1440–1620 · bars 13–14(½) · BLUE · 2D kinetic
The one blue ground in the film. Enormous white type, word by word from line masks:
**"Hiring, decided on merit."** Bleeding white bars slice through between words.
- SFX: riser resolves into the biggest hit of the film on the downbeat.

### S8 · End card — 0:27.0–0:30.0 · f 1620–1800 · bars 14(½)–15 · white · logo
White ground. The official wordmark in ink `#1F2430` builds glyph by glyph, then the tie drops into
the open slot with a single swing and a click. Accent rule draws under it, tagline sets beneath.
Hold for 1.2 s. Cut to black on the last frame.
- VO: **"HireHouse."** lands with the tie.
- SFX: tie swish → click → logo sting with a clean tail.
